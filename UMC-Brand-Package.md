## 4. Voice & Language

The Unbound Minds Collective uses intentional, community-grounded language. Please match this tone in any subdomain content.

**Use these words:**
- Inclusive, Witnessing, Acknowledges, Respects, Center, Empowerment

**Use these terms:**
- Community-Grounded, Non-pathologizing, Subculture-informed, Radical welcome
- Holding space, Emotional acknowledgement, Lived-experience focused
- Strengths-based, Peer-led, Self-Determination, Liberation-focused
- De-stigmatized, Depolarized, Open-container

**Avoid:**
- "Affirming" (overused and imprecise — replace with the above)
- Clinical or diagnostic language
- Any implication that community members need to be "fixed"

**Example phrases:**
- "We honor your autonomy"
- "A container designed explicitly to welcome diverse relationship structures and identities"
- "Built by and for the LGBTQ+, kink, and non-monogamous communities"
- "Where alternative relationship styles and identities are fully seen and respected"
- "Centering and honoring marginalized adult narratives"
- "Holding an intentional, moderated space for your whole self"

## 7. Key Design Principles

- **Mobile-first** — all layouts designed for 375px, 768px, 1280px breakpoints
- **Warm, earthy aesthetic** — nothing cold, clinical, or corporate
- **High contrast** — near-black text on warm off-white background throughout
- **Rounded corners** — `rounded-sm` (Tailwind v4 convention)
- **Subtle shadows** — `shadow-sm` only
- **No hard borders** — use surface colour changes to separate sections

---

## 8. Important Platform Notes

- This is a **peer advocacy platform — not therapy, not crisis intervention**
- Always include the disclaimer: *"This platform does not provide therapy, crisis intervention, or professional mental health services."*
- Crisis resources are always linked prominently (see `/crisis`)
- Member data is private — never display email addresses publicly
- Content in the community forum may be sensitive — handle with care

## 9. Subdomain Integration

If building on a subdomain (e.g. `contributors.unboundmindscollective.org`):

- Match the colour palette exactly (Section 2)
- Use the same typefaces (Section 3)
- Match the voice and avoid "affirming" language (Section 4)
- Link back to the main site in your header or footer
- Use the full logo or the wordmark — available on request from Amber
